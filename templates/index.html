Questions

Frequently asked.

Is the free scan really free? What's the catch? +
Genuinely free. No credit card, no hidden tier. We run your CV through the same parsing engine we use for paid reports and send you the top-level results. The business model is that a meaningful percentage of people want more detail than the free scan gives, and they upgrade. Most don't, and that's fine.
How accurate is your scoring? +
Our engine models the logic used by the major ATS platforms — Workday, Greenhouse, Oracle Taleo, iCIMS — which together account for around 80% of mid-to-large employer deployments globally. No tool can exactly replicate a specific employer's configuration, but the structural issues we flag (text boxes, tables, headers, column layouts) cause parsing failures across virtually every modern ATS.
Does this work for UK and US job markets too? +
Yes. The R99 Full Diagnostic Report includes region tuning for South Africa, the United Kingdom, and the United States. ATS systems themselves are broadly the same worldwide, but keyword conventions and CV formatting expectations differ by region. We automatically detect the target region from your job description or let you specify it.
Who's behind Cited? +
Cited is a small South African team working on CV optimisation and ATS research. We deliberately don't front the service with an individual name — the scoring engine, the templates, and the research are what matter to you. Contracts and invoices carry a registered entity; feel free to ask if you need that detail before engaging the rewrite service.
What happens to my CV data? +
Your CV is processed to generate your score and report, then stored encrypted for 30 days so you can retrieve or upgrade your report within that window. After 30 days it's permanently deleted. We never share CVs with third parties, never train AI models on them, and never sell contact data. The full policy is on our POPIA page.
Can I get a refund on the rewrite? +
If your rewritten CV doesn't score at least 20 points higher than your original on our scan, we'll refund the rewrite in full. In practice this has never happened — average lifts are +31 points — but the guarantee is real and in the contract.
